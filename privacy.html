Privacy policy

Version 1 · effective when published · applies to the RanchHub desktop app and this website.

The short version: RanchHub runs on your own computer. Your data stays there. Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on a feature that needs it, and this page says exactly which features those are.

Who we are

RanchHub is published by Doug Glendenning, Liberty Lake, Washington, USA. Questions about privacy: getranchhub@gmail.com.

What RanchHub stores, and where

  • Your data file. Jobs, the brake, your gates, the wait list, held packets and (if you use it) your chat history are kept in one file on your computer: ranchhub-data.json. Installed from the Microsoft Store it lives in your user folder under …\Packages\…\LocalState\; run from a folder it sits beside the app.
  • Audit log. Every Clean Exchange check writes one line to ranchhub-exchange-audit.jsonl beside the data file. It holds times, directions, item types and rule names – never the contents of a packet.
  • Cost ledger. If you turn on Talk to Bex, each call's cost is one line in chat-ledger.csv beside the data file.

These files are yours. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app leaves your data file in place so you can keep or delete it.

What RanchHub does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no license check.
  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reports or usage statistics. The app never contacts us.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data.
  • The dashboard is served only to your own computer (127.0.0.1); other devices on your network cannot reach it.

What leaves your computer – only when you turn it on

  • Talk to Bex. If you add your own Anthropic API key, what you type to Bex, a short summary of the hub (brake line, gates, open jobs, wait list, how many packets are held) and the results of the tools Bex uses are sent to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic's privacy policy and API terms apply to that data. Your key is read once at start-up, is never written, shown, logged or sent anywhere except in the request to Anthropic, and you can remove it at any time.
  • Push to talk and read aloud. These use your web browser's own speech features. Chrome sends the audio to Google and Edge sends it to Microsoft to turn it into text, under their privacy policies. Read aloud uses a voice installed on your computer; if your browser has none for your language it may use an online voice, and the reply text then goes to that browser's provider.
  • Jimmy inbox folder. If you choose a folder for messages to Jimmy, RanchHub writes one text file there per message. If that folder syncs to a cloud service (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox), the files go with it under that service's terms.
  • Clean Exchange. Packets you place in your exchange folder are checked on your computer. Nothing is uploaded by RanchHub; what your exchange folder itself syncs is governed by the service it syncs with.
  • Microsoft Store. When you install or update RanchHub from the Store, Microsoft processes the install under its own privacy statement. We receive no personal data from the Store, only aggregate counts.

This website

getranchhub.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record your IP address in its server logs under the GitHub privacy statement. The home page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sends your IP address to Google. We set no cookies and run no analytics.

Children

RanchHub is a tool for working professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Everything above that leaves your computer is off until you turn it on, and off again when you remove the key, folder or feature. To delete all RanchHub data, uninstall the app and delete the data file, audit log and ledger named above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new version number and date. Material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.
